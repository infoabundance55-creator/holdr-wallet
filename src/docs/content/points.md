# Points · Season 1

Holdr Points reward real use: trading, holding, launching and playing. Season 1 lasts three months and starts with the launchpad.

> **Coming soon** These are the planned Season 1 rules. They may change before the season starts, and each season publishes its own rules.

> **Note** Points have no use and no promised value. They are a record and a ranking. Holdr makes no promise of a token, an airdrop or any reward for points.

## How to earn

| Activity | Points | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| Daily check-in | 100 | Once a day |
| 7-day check-in streak | +500 | Once every 7 days |
| Moon Jump coins | +5 per coin up to 1,000 m; +10 per coin above | 3 scored runs a day, all added up |
| Moon Jump daily challenge | Top 10: +2,000 · Top 100: +500 | Ranked by your best run of the day |
| Trading | 10 per $1 traded | Minimum $5 per trade; lower rate on large volume (below) |
| Graduation witness | +2,000 | You held the token for over 24 hours when it graduated; once per token |
| Holding (HODL) | √(value) × 10 × days bonus | Up to 2,000 per token per day |
| Launching a token | 1,000 | Counts once 5 unrelated wallets have bought; up to 3 tokens a day |
| Your token reaches $1,000 volume | +5,000 | Volume from unrelated wallets only |
| Your token graduates | +30,000 | At least 20 different holders at graduation |
| Referrals | 5% of your friends' points | Paid by Holdr, not taken from friends |

Launch points are capped at 36,000 per token.

## Trading

| Your trading that day | Points per $1 |
|---|---|
| First $1,000 | 10 |
| $1,000 to $10,000 | 5 |
| Above $10,000 | 2.5 |

For example, trading $100 earns 1,000 points and $2,000 earns 15,000. Buying and selling the same token back and forth between related wallets earns nothing.

## Holding

Holding points use the **lowest** amount of a token you held during the day (00:00 to 24:00), valued at that day's volume-weighted average price:

**points per token per day = √(lowest value that day) × 10 × days bonus**

| Days held | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Days 1 to 6 | ×1 |
| Days 7 to 29 | ×1.5 |
| Day 30 onwards | ×2 |

| Holding value | Days 1–6 | Days 7–29 | Day 30+ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $20 | 45 | 67 | 89 |
| $100 | 100 | 150 | 200 |
| $1,000 | 316 | 474 | 632 |
| $10,000 | 1,000 | 1,500 | 2,000 (cap) |

- Only tokens you bought count; tokens transferred in from other wallets don't.
- Tokens bought today start counting after a full day of holding.
- If your lowest holding drops by more than half from the day before, the day count restarts.

## Referrals

- Share your personal invite link. A friend is linked to you the first time they sign in, permanently.
- You get **5%** of your friend's points for their first 90 days, one level only. Holdr pays it on top; your friend loses nothing.
- It counts once your friend has traded **$20** in the season. Your friend also gets a **500-point** welcome bonus.

## Character bonuses

A bound Genesis character adds +20% to one kind of points, or +10% to all. See [Genesis characters](/docs/genesis-characters).

## How points are credited

- Points are calculated once a day from on-chain data, so anyone can check them.
- New points show as **pending** for 24 to 72 hours before they are confirmed. Points from cheating are removed.
- Check-in, game and referral points need the wallet to have traded **$20** in the season first. Until then they wait as pending.
- New wallets (under 7 days old, or with no other on-chain history) earn half points.

## Fair play

Points reward one person's real activity. Wallets that share funding, were created together or act the same way count as one person, and trades between them earn nothing. The rules are public; the detection details are not.
