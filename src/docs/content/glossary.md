# Glossary

**Bonding curve.** A pool that always quotes a price from a formula. On Holdrs, price = x ÷ y and x × y never goes down.

**Curve pool.** The contract that holds a token's unsold supply and its USDG before graduation. One per token.

**Graduation.** The moment a curve pool reaches 10,000 USDG and the token moves to Uniswap v4 with locked liquidity.

**Locked liquidity.** Uniswap liquidity that nobody can remove, ever. Holdrs' locker has no function to withdraw it.

**Hook.** A small contract attached to a Uniswap v4 pool. Holdrs' hook only controls who can create the official pool.

**Anti-snipe surcharge.** An extra charge on buys in the first 5 seconds after launch. It stays in the token's pool.

**USDG.** Global Dollar, a US-dollar stablecoin issued by Paxos. Holdrs' launchpad prices everything in USDG.

**Gas.** The network fee for a transaction, paid in ETH on Robinhood Chain.

**Recovery phrase.** The 12 (or 24) words that are your wallet. Whoever has them controls the funds.

**Self-custody.** Only you hold your keys, in your own wallet. Holdrs can't move, freeze or recover your funds.

**Testnet.** A copy of the network for testing, where tokens have no value.

**Safe multisig.** A wallet that needs several keys to approve an action. Holdrs' admin is a 2-of-3 Safe.

**Timelock.** A required wait before a change takes effect, so everyone can see it coming. Holdrs' rule changes wait 48 hours.

**Commit-reveal.** A way to draw randomly where one side commits to a hidden value first, so nobody can pick the result.

**Season.** A points period. Season 1 lasts three months.
