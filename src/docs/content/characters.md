# Characters

Holdrs has four characters. They're free: you unlock them by earning points, and your character levels up with your house in HODL Town.

> **Coming soon** Characters arrive with sign-in and Season 1.

## Levels

Your level follows your **lifetime points** (all seasons added up). Each level brings a new house, and some bring a new character and a points bonus.

| Level | Lifetime points | House | Title | Character | Bonus |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | Tent | Fresh Wallet | Degen Raccoon | +0% |
| 2 | 10,000 | Cabin | Dip Buyer | Degen Raccoon | +0% |
| 3 | 50,000 | Stone House | HODLer | **Moon Frog** | +2% |
| 4 | 200,000 | Villa | Bull Rider | **Laser Bull** | +4% |
| 5 | 1,000,000 | Tower | Whale | **The Anon** | +8% |
| 6 | 5,000,000 | Moon Base | Moon Lord | The Anon | +8% |

## Bonus

- The bonus applies to **all your points except referral points**.
- It uses your level at 00:00 each day: level up today, and the new bonus starts tomorrow.
- Levels are permanent. You never drop a level when a season ends.

## Your look

- Every character you've unlocked can be your look: your Moon Jump player, the character at your door in HODL Town and your avatar.
- Change it any time with one tap. Your look never changes your bonus.
- By default you use your current level's character.

## Not NFTs

Characters live on your Holdrs account, linked to your wallet address. They are not NFTs: there is nothing to mint, buy, sell or trade, and no gas to pay. To get one, sign in and pick a username; everyone starts as Degen Raccoon.

Characters and points have no promised value. See [Points](/docs/points).
