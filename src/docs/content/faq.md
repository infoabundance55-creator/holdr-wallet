# FAQ

## Is Holdr live?

The wallet is live. The launchpad contracts are on Robinhood Chain testnet and the launchpad app is coming. Points, Genesis characters and Moon Jump follow. See [Introduction](/docs/introduction).

## Can Holdr access my funds?

No. Your keys stay on your device, encrypted with your password. Holdr can't move, freeze or recover your wallet. See [Wallet security](/docs/wallet-security).

## I lost my recovery phrase. Can you help?

No one can. If you still have access on a device, open **Me → View recovery phrase** now and write it down. If you have lost both, the wallet can't be restored.

## Can a token creator rug the liquidity?

No. Before graduation, USDG sits in the token's curve pool and only buyers and sellers move it. After graduation the liquidity is locked forever and nobody, including Holdr, can remove it. Creators can still sell tokens they own, like anyone else.

## Why did my buy get fewer tokens than expected?

Either the price moved before your trade landed (your slippage setting protects you), or you bought in the first 5 seconds after launch and paid the anti-snipe surcharge. See [Anti-snipe](/docs/anti-snipe).

## Why did my buy only use part of my USDG?

Your buy graduated the token. The graduating buy only takes what is needed to reach 10,000 USDG. See [Graduation](/docs/graduation).

## Can I sell if the launchpad is paused?

Yes. A pause only stops launches and buys. Selling and claiming always work.

## Are points worth money?

No. Points have no use and no promised value; they are a record and a ranking. See [Points](/docs/points).

## Where can I check the contracts?

See [Contract addresses](/docs/contracts) and the [source code on GitHub](https://github.com/infoabundance55-creator/holdr-wallet/tree/contracts/contracts).

## How do I report a bug?

Report security issues privately as described in [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/infoabundance55-creator/holdr-wallet/blob/main/SECURITY.md). For everything else, reach us on X at [@holdr_app](https://x.com/holdr_app).
