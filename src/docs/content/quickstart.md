# Quickstart

Connect your wallet, get free test funds and make your first trade. It takes about five minutes and costs nothing.

## 1. Get a wallet

Holdrs works with the wallet you already use. On desktop, use a browser extension such as **MetaMask**, **Rabby**, **OKX Wallet** or **Coinbase Wallet**. On a phone, scan a QR code with WalletConnect, or open Holdrs in your wallet app's built-in browser.

New to wallets? See [Install a wallet and add Robinhood Chain](/docs/wallet-setup).

## 2. Connect

1. Open [holdrs.xyz](https://holdrs.xyz).
2. Tap **Connect wallet** (top right) and pick your wallet.
3. Approve the connection in your wallet.

Holdrs runs on **Robinhood Chain testnet**. If your wallet is on another network, Holdrs shows a bar with **Switch network**. Tap it; if your wallet doesn't have the network yet, it asks you to add it. See [Testnet](/docs/testnet) for the network details.

> **Note** Connecting only shares your address. Holdrs never sees your recovery phrase and can't move your funds. Every trade needs your approval in your wallet.

## 3. Get test ETH

ETH pays the network fee (gas) for every transaction. Open the [Alchemy Robinhood testnet faucet](https://www.alchemy.com/faucets/robinhood-testnet), paste your address and get 0.1 test ETH a day.

> **Note** The faucet asks that the address has a little ETH and some history on Ethereum mainnet. If yours isn't eligible, use a wallet that is, then send the test ETH to the wallet you use on Holdrs.

## 4. Get test USDG

The launchpad prices everything in USDG. On testnet, Holdrs runs a free **Test USDG**. Tap **Get free test USDG** on any token's trade panel or on the **Me** page. It is also available on the [Test USDG contract](https://explorer.testnet.chain.robinhood.com/address/0xf820a6e153452B6699Ce9BD794C78E644284F033) (`faucet(amount)`, 6 decimals).

## 5. Trade

Pick a token on **Discover**, enter an amount and tap **Buy**. Your wallet asks you to approve the exact USDG amount for this trade, then to confirm the trade.
