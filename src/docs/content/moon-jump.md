# Moon Jump

Moon Jump is Holdrs' 32-bit arcade game. Jump up through the market, dodge the bears and collect coins. **Coins are points.**

> **Coming soon** Moon Jump opens with Season 1. Sign in with your wallet to play; no NFT needed.

## Scoring

| Height | Each coin |
|---|---|
| 0 to 1,000 m | +5 points |
| Above 1,000 m (bull market) | +10 points |

- **3 scored runs a day.** Coins from all three are added up, with no cap. Restarting a run uses one.
- **Practice mode** is unlimited and scores nothing.
- Height isn't scored, but you see which stage you've reached.

## Leaderboards

| Board | Ranks |
|---|---|
| Today | The daily challenge: everyone plays the same map, best run of the day. Top 10 get +2,000 points, top 100 get +500. |
| Season | Total coins collected this season |

## Fair play

Runs are checked on the server by replaying them before game points are confirmed. Your character is your look only; every character plays the same, so it's pure skill.
