# Welcome to Holdrs

Holdrs is a token launchpad on Robinhood Chain. **Click to launch · Hold to earn.** Launch a token in a minute, trade it on a bonding curve, and earn points for trading, holding and playing. Connect the wallet you already use: MetaMask, Rabby, OKX or any WalletConnect wallet.

> **Testnet** Holdrs is in public testing on Robinhood Chain testnet. Test tokens have no value. The contracts have had an independent AI security review but no paid audit yet. Don't hold meaningful funds on mainnet yet.

## What you can do

| | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|

| **Points · Season 1** | Earn points for check-ins, trading, holding, launching and playing. | Coming soon |
| **Characters** | Four free characters that level up with your points and add a points bonus. | Coming soon |
| **Moon Jump** | A 32-bit arcade game where coins are points. | Coming soon |

## How the money moves

1. **Launch.** A creator launches a token. All 1,000,000,000 tokens start in the token's own curve pool.
2. **Trade on the curve.** Buyers pay USDG into the pool and receive tokens; sellers return tokens and get USDG back. Each trade pays a 1% fee: 70% to the creator, 30% to Holdrs.
3. **Graduate.** When the pool holds 10,000 USDG, the token graduates in the same transaction: 200,000,000 tokens and the 10,000 USDG become a Uniswap v4 pool whose liquidity is locked forever.
4. **Trade on Uniswap.** The pool keeps charging 1%, still split 70 / 30.

Nobody, including Holdrs, can take the USDG out of a curve pool or remove the locked liquidity. See [Admin controls](/docs/admin-controls) for exactly what Holdrs can and can't do.
