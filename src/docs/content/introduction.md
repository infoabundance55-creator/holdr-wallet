# Welcome to Holdr

Holdr is a self-custody wallet and token launchpad on Robinhood Chain. Hold crypto and tokenized stocks, launch a token in a minute, and earn points for trading, holding and playing.

> **Testnet** Holdr is in public testing on Robinhood Chain testnet. Test tokens have no value. The wallet is v0.1 and the contracts have had an independent AI security review but no paid audit yet. Don't hold meaningful funds on mainnet yet.

## What you can do

| | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Wallet** | Create or import a wallet, send and receive. Your keys never leave your device. | Live |
| **Launchpad** | Launch a token on a bonding curve priced in USDG. At 10,000 USDG it graduates to Uniswap with liquidity locked forever. | Contracts live on testnet, app coming |
| **Points · Season 1** | Earn points for check-ins, trading, holding, launching and playing. | Coming soon |
| **Genesis characters** | 5,000 free-mint characters that boost your points and unlock Moon Jump. | Coming soon |
| **Moon Jump** | A 32-bit arcade game where coins are points. | Coming soon |

## How the money moves

1. **Launch.** A creator launches a token. All 1,000,000,000 tokens start in the token's own curve pool.
2. **Trade on the curve.** Buyers pay USDG into the pool and receive tokens; sellers return tokens and get USDG back. Each trade pays a 1% fee: 70% to the creator, 30% to Holdr.
3. **Graduate.** When the pool holds 10,000 USDG, the token graduates in the same transaction: 200,000,000 tokens and the 10,000 USDG become a Uniswap v4 pool whose liquidity is locked forever.
4. **Trade on Uniswap.** The pool keeps charging 1%, still split 70 / 30.

Nobody, including Holdr, can take the USDG out of a curve pool or remove the locked liquidity. See [Admin controls](/docs/admin-controls) for exactly what Holdr can and can't do.
